TraceXMail Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Lead Developer / Controller: Jayram Sappa (jayramsappa537@gmail.com)

1. Overview

TraceXMail is an email security and forensic threat analysis application designed to help cybersecurity operators and analysts inspect raw email headers, verify cryptographic authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, ARC), reconstruct hop traceroutes, and detect phishing threats.

2. Scope of Ingested Information

TraceXMail processes email artifacts uploaded by users (RFC 822 .eml files, raw email headers, message IDs, and defanged threat indicators). Ingested forensic data is used solely to generate security dossiers and forensic metrics.

3. Google OAuth & Limited Use Policy Compliance

Google API User Data Policy Compliance:

TraceXMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell user data to third parties, advertising brokers, or data aggregators.
  • We do not use or transfer user email data for serving ads, personalized marketing, or retargeting.
  • We do not allow humans to read user email data unless required by law, with explicit user consent for debugging, or in aggregated, anonymized form for security operations.
