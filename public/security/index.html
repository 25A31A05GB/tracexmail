TraceXMail Security Architecture & Disclosure

Effective Date: September 2026 | Lead Architect: Jayram Sappa (jayramsappa537@gmail.com)

1. Security Architecture

TraceXMail employs multi-layered defenses: all traffic is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption, sensitive database records are secured with AES-256 encryption at rest, and email previews are isolated in sandboxed iframe environments.

2. Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure & Safe Harbor

Vulnerability Reporting:

Send findings to jayramsappa537@gmail.com.

Safe Harbor Guarantee: We commit not to initiate legal action against ethical security researchers conducting good-faith security testing who avoid user privacy violations and provide reasonable remediation time.
