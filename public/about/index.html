About TraceXMail

TraceXMail is a dedicated cybersecurity intelligence and digital forensics platform engineered for security operations center (SOC) analysts, incident responders, corporate IT administrators, and security researchers. Our platform deconstructs suspicious email messages down to their raw RFC 822 cryptographic headers, identifies spoofed relay hops, computes MTA latency, and correlates threat vectors against the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

1. Our Mission: Eliminating Hallucinations in Email Forensics

In contemporary cyber warfare, email remains the primary initial access vector (accounting for over 85% of corporate breaches according to industry threat telemetry). Traditional security gateways and basic mail clients often summarize or strip the underlying header artifacts that reveal the true origin of a lure.

TraceXMail was created to provide deterministic, transparent, and reproducible forensic analysis. Every score, verdict, and hop traceroute generated by our platform is tied to bit-exact evidence, rigorous RFC specifications, and cryptographically verifiable public key DNS records.

RFC 5322 & RFC 822
Rigorous parsing of Internet Message Format, Received hop chains, Return-Path, and envelope boundaries.
RFC 7208 (SPF)
Sender Policy Framework validation, evaluating envelope MAIL FROM domains against DNS TXT records.
RFC 6376 (DKIM)
DomainKeys Identified Mail cryptographic signature verification across header canonicalization algorithms.
RFC 7489 (DMARC)
Domain-based Message Authentication Reporting and Conformance alignment check for strict and relaxed modes.

3. Core Architectural Commitments

  • Zero-Retention Client Enclave: Header parsing and hop deconstruction occur in a private sandbox. User evidence is never used to train public machine learning models.
  • Court-Admissible Evidence Standards: Automated generation of FRE 902 / ISO 27037 digital custody certificates with bit-exact SHA-256 digests.
  • Safe Visual Detonation: Micro-sandboxed DOM isolation prevents active script execution, web bugs, tracking pixels, and payload triggers during inspection.
  • Open Knowledge Contribution: We publish free, accessible technical guides on email authentication protocols, header anatomy, and incident response playbooks for the global cybersecurity ecosystem.
