Automated Email Forensic Deconstruction & Threat Intelligence

TraceXMail provides defensive cybersecurity practitioners, incident response teams, and SOC analysts with an end-to-end investigation suite for deconstructing phishing lures, business email compromise (BEC), and spoofed email headers. Unlike traditional email security solutions that output opaque verdicts, TraceXMail provides transparent, reproducible, and court-admissible forensic dossiers backed by bit-exact SHA-256 evidence digests.

Core Capabilities & Technical Architecture

Safe Visual Detonation Sandbox

Zero-script, DOM-isolated sandbox iframe defangs tracking pixels, web beacons, and malicious JavaScript payloads while preserving visual fidelity for analysts.

MTA Hop Traceroute Chronology

Dissects every RFC 5322 Received header, resolves autonomous system numbers (ASN), computes server transfer latencies in milliseconds, and detects injected forged hops.

Cryptographic Protocol Validation

Automated verification of Sender Policy Framework (RFC 7208), DomainKeys Identified Mail (RFC 6376), and DMARC alignment (RFC 7489) in strict and relaxed modes.

MITRE ATT&CK Matrix Correlation

Maps email threat indicators to enterprise attack tactics (T1566 Phishing, T1586 Compromised Accounts, T1534 Internal Spearphishing) with actionable mitigation guidance.
